Anchored
This card belongs to a fixed home location or part of Nadir and cannot come to rest elsewhere through ordinary placement.

Hydration
How well hydrated Nadir is. Reaching zero is fatal.

Satiation
How well fed Nadir is. Reaching zero is fatal.

Vision
How well Nadir can see well enough to act. Room light and active equipment modify the effective value used for tasks, searching, and travel.

Infection
How infected a wound is. Lower is better.

Dressed
The wound has been covered with a sterile dressing.

Sterilized
This material has been sterilized and can be used where sterile fabric is required.

Fabric
This card can be used as fabric in interactions that require it.

Container
This card can hold a substance such as water.

Contains-Water
This container currently contains water.

Cutting Tool
This card can perform interactions that require a cutting tool.

Durability
How much usable life remains in the item before wear makes it unusable.

Spoilage
How far food or organic material has progressed toward spoiling.

Fuel
How much usable fuel remains.

Battery
How much battery charge remains.

Locked
This object is currently locked.

Powered
This object currently has electrical power.

Progress
How far an ongoing Process has progressed toward completion.
