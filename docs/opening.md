# Opening evacuation

This document records the current decided opening play experience for Safe Room.

## DECIDED BY SIMON

The game opens with a short evacuation interlude before the main survival/exploration phase.

The authorities are coming and Nadir has to leave. The player chooses what to grab by moving cards from the opening room into Nadir's Inventory before escaping.

The normal Inventory capacity should create the choice rather than a special one-off "choose N items" rule. For now, Nadir can fill his ordinary Inventory before leaving.

After the escape, the main game begins in **Tunnels**. Nadir does not begin with an established base of operations and must later find somewhere suitable to use as one.

### Items available in the opening room

The current opening offer is:

- `Pocket Knife` ×1 — a very basic knife and poor weapon. It can be used to make wood shavings from wood; the shavings can be used as kindling.
- `Plastic Bottle` ×2 — both start containing water.
- `Canned Food` ×2.
- `T-Shirt` ×1 — can be ripped into `Fabric`.
- `Simple Lighter` ×1 — starts half empty.
- `Flashlight` ×1 — starts with 20% battery. A flashlight is required for going to the Deep Tunnels.
- `Spare Batteries` ×1 — for the flashlight.
- `Pain Killers` ×1.
- `Simple Backpack` ×1 — starts empty. Once Nadir is in the Tunnels, it allows him to carry five additional cards. Cards carried inside the backpack do **not** count as equipped.

This is currently 11 offered card instances competing for the normal five-card Inventory capacity. The player can therefore fill Inventory, but cannot take everything.

The starting loadout is intentionally player-authored. Different choices should emphasize different immediate capabilities rather than there being one fixed designer-issued starter kit.

## OPEN

The following details are not yet fixed:

- the exact name and physical/narrative identity of the opening room;
- whether the opening room can ever be revisited;
- how urgency is presented mechanically or visually during the interlude;
- whether the player can put cards into the backpack during the opening or only after arriving in Tunnels;
- exact Values/Markers for lighter fuel, flashlight charge, batteries, pain killers, clothing, and backpack capacity;
- whether any of the listed quantities or offered items change after playtesting.
